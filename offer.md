# SaaS Stack Auditor — Offer Document
*Created: 2026-03-29*

---

## THE PROBLEM

Small businesses (5–50 employees) are bleeding money on SaaS they don't fully use.

**The numbers:**
- Average 5–20 person company runs 15–25 SaaS tools
- Average monthly SaaS spend: $3,000–$5,000/mo
- Estimated waste (redundancy + unused seats): 25–40% = **$750–$2,000/mo thrown away**
- Nobody is tracking this. The CEO doesn't know. The ops manager doesn't know. Finance sees 25 line items and no one questions them.

**Why it happens:**
- Tools get added one at a time ("just $49/mo, no big deal")
- No one owns the audit — it falls between ops, finance, and IT
- Switching feels risky, so nothing gets cut
- Vendors make it intentionally hard to cancel or compare

**The result:** Companies are paying for 3 project management tools, 2 CRMs, 4 communication platforms, and a handful of tools nobody's opened in 6 months.

---

## THE SOLUTION

A done-for-you SaaS audit that tells you exactly what to cut, what to consolidate, and what you're actually saving — with the math done for you.

**How it works:**
1. Customer fills out a 10-minute intake form (list of tools + monthly costs + team size + use cases)
2. We analyze for redundancy, underutilization, and consolidation opportunities
3. Deliver a clean report within 48–72 hours:
   - Full stack map (what you have, what it costs, what it does)
   - Redundancy findings (where you're paying twice for the same thing)
   - Recommended cuts (with estimated savings)
   - Recommended consolidations (tools that replace 2–3 others)
   - ROI math (cost of audit vs annual savings)
   - Optional: priority-ordered action plan

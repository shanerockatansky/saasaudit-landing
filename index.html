FAQ

Common questions

How long does it take?

Uploading your billing export takes about 5 minutes. The audit report is delivered within 48 hours.

Do you need access to our AWS/Azure/GCP account?

No. Just a billing CSV or PDF export from the last 3 months. No credentials, no read access, no integrations. You export the file, we analyze it.

Which cloud providers do you support?

AWS, Azure, and GCP. If you're split across multiple providers, send all three — we'll analyze them together.

What size company is this for?

Best fit for companies spending $10,000 or more per month on cloud. Below that threshold, the savings typically don't justify the audit cost — and we'll tell you that upfront.

Our engineering team already did a cost review last year.

That's worth knowing. If it was recent and thorough, we may not find much — and we'd rather tell you that than take your money. But in most cases, a year is long enough for significant new waste to accumulate as infrastructure changes.

Will implementing the recommendations require engineering work?

Most findings don't require code changes — they're configuration changes, instance resizes, or service shutdowns. We flag anything that requires engineering involvement and estimate the effort so you can prioritize.

Can't we do this ourselves?

You can. It takes 4–8 hours of a senior engineer's time, plus the context to know what normal looks like across different company sizes and workload patterns. We do this every week — we pattern-match across hundreds of bills. You get that without pulling your team off feature work.
